Mathematical Formulation & Step-by-Step Derivation

A simply supported beam under a uniformly distributed load (UDL) is statically determinate. The equations are derived using classical Newtonian static equilibrium and the Euler-Bernoulli beam theory:

1. Support Reaction Forces (Auflagerkräfte)
R_A = R_B = \frac{w \cdot L}{2}
Derived from vertical equilibrium \(\sum F_z = 0\) and moment equilibrium \(\sum M_A = 0\).
2. Shear Force Equation (Querkraftfunktion)
V(x) = w \cdot \left(\frac{L}{2} - x\right)
Linear gradient with maximum \(V_{\max} = \frac{wL}{2}\) at supports and zero crossing at \(x = L/2\).
3. Bending Moment Equation (Biegemoment)
M(x) = \frac{w \cdot x}{2} \cdot (L - x)
Quadratic parabola. Peak at \(x = L/2\): \(M_{\max} = \frac{w \cdot L^2}{8}\).
4. Elastic Deflection (Euler-Bernoulli)
\delta(x) = \frac{w \cdot x}{24 E I} \left(L^3 - 2 L x^2 + x^3\right)
Peak deflection at midspan: \(\delta_{\max} = \frac{5 \cdot w \cdot L^4}{384 \cdot E \cdot I}\).
5. Support Rotation Angle (Verdrehwinkel)
\theta_A = \theta_B = \frac{w \cdot L^3}{24 \cdot E \cdot I}
Slope of the elastic curve at bearings in radians.
6. Maximum Bending Stress (Biegespannung)
\sigma_{\max} = \frac{M_{\max}}{W_y} = \frac{M_{\max} \cdot (h / 2)}{I_y}
Extreme fiber tensile & compressive stress in outer fibers.

CLOSED-FORM REFERENCE SHEET

Exact analytical solutions for a prismatic beam (constant EI) under uniform load w.

1 · SUPPORT REACTIONSΣFy = 0, ΣMA = 0
$$R_A = R_B = \frac{w \cdot L}{2}$$
2 · SHEAR FORCELinear · V(L/2) = 0
$$V(x) = w\left(\frac{L}{2} - x\right), \qquad V_{\max} = \pm\frac{wL}{2}$$
3 · BENDING MOMENTParabolic · Peak at L/2
$$M(x) = \frac{wx}{2}(L - x), \qquad M_{\max} = \frac{wL^2}{8}$$
4 · ELASTIC DEFLECTION4th-Order Polynomial
$$\delta(x) = \frac{wx}{24EI}\left(L^3 - 2Lx^2 + x^3\right), \quad \delta_{\max} = \frac{5wL^4}{384EI}$$
5 · SUPPORT ROTATION ANGLESlope at A & B (rad)
$$\theta_A = \theta_B = \frac{wL^3}{24EI}$$
6 · EXTREME FIBER BENDING STRESSNavier’s Formula
$$\sigma_{\max} = \frac{M_{\max}}{W_y} = \frac{M_{\max}\cdot (h/2)}{I_y}$$

Formulas: Cutting Speed

Known physical quantities are substituted into the formula together with their units. Before or during calculation, units are converted so the arithmetic works consistently and the result obtains the required target unit. The result is always a numerical value with a unit.

Cutting speed vc

Cutting speed is the tangential speed at the circumference: in one revolution a point on the circumference travels distance π · d, and in one minute n times that distance. Rotation is shown slowed down 50×.

Components of a Formula

  • Operators (arithmetic rules), e.g., · for multiplication, fraction bar for division
  • Constants, e.g., π = 3.14159 …
  • Numbers, here the substituted numerical values and unit conversion factors

Numerical-Value Equations: Motor Torque

In numerical-value equations, standard unit conversions are already built into the numerical constant. Values of each quantity may only be substituted in their prescribed units. Units are not carried through the calculation; the unit of the target quantity is predetermined.

Cost Function, Revenue Function, and Break-Even Point

Total cost KG and revenue E are linear functions of quantity M. Where both lines intersect lies the break-even point (BEP): here KG = E. Smaller quantities result in a loss, larger quantities in a profit. Clicking or dragging in the diagram adjusts quantity M.

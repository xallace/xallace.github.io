Methodology, Standards & Independence

DIN 461 & DIN EN ISO 80000-2 Axis labeling, unit brackets or placement between the last two scale numbers, line-width ratios of 1 : 2 : 4, and positive angle orientation in polar coordinates follow the standards cited in the book.
Original & Copyright-Clean All explanations, text, and SVG/Canvas graphics are independently programmed. Only the underlying rules and numerical parameters of the worked examples are drawn from Tabellenbuch Metall (48th ed.).
100 % Offline & Deterministically Tested All six applications run directly in the browser with no backend and no external runtime dependencies. The calculation cores for p. 14 (49 checks), p. 58 (41 checks), p. 59 (52 checks), p. 60 (62 checks), p. 61 (24 checks), and p. 62 (25 checks) are verified against the textbook values via Node test scripts.

KfzDyn Vehicle Dynamics Mitschke/Wallentowitz, 5th ed. Simulation Models

Interactive models for tires, driving performance, braking, vibrations, and handling. The models are implemented from the textbook equations and verified against its numerical examples.

Source of models and example data: M. Mitschke, H. Wallentowitz: Dynamik der Kraftfahrzeuge, 5th ed., Springer Vieweg 2014. Equation numbers and page references refer to this edition. Textbook prose and figures are not reproduced; chart curves are digitized only where the book does not state an explicit formula (see the “Verification & Sources” tab).

    How the Frame Is Structured

    • 1st compartment: Tolerance characteristic symbol
    • 2nd compartment: Tolerance value in mm, Ø for a cylindrical or circular zone, followed by modifiers such as Ⓜ, Ⓛ, CZ
    • 3rd to 5th compartments: Datums in primary, secondary, tertiary order. A-B in one compartment denotes a common datum
    • Datum letters: Uppercase letters, excluding I, O, Q, X (ISO 5459)
